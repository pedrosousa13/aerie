Ad and tracker blocking, phishing and malware protection, fingerprinting
protection, the hardening settings and Workspaces isolation lower your risk.
They do not remove it. Filter and phishing lists miss things and block things
by mistake, and a determined site can still find ways to track you. Nothing
in Aerie guarantees that you are anonymous or safe.
